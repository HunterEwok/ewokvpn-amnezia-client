Get your own self-hosted VPN!

ewokVPN is a multi-protocol, open-source VPN client that offers the option to set up your own server. It is a fork of AmneziaVPN (https://github.com/amnezia-vpn/amnezia-client) and is not affiliated with the AmneziaVPN team.

Free open-source software to create a personal VPN on your server. Helps to access blocked content without revealing privacy even to VPN providers. Specify IP address, credentials for your server and ewokVPN will configure it to connect via VPN automatically.

You can use your own self-hosted VPS or purchase any VPS from any provider.

Source code (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/HunterEwok/ewokvpn-amnezia-client
